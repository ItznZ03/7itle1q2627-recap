Unit 1 • Foundation

Introduction to ICT & Entrepreneurship

Information and Communication Technology (ICT) powers modern industry, driving global innovation and daily communication. Unit 1 covers foundational tech sectors, potential career trajectories, key business opportunities, and fundamental digital principles.

Core Pillars

Components of ICT

Hardware

Examples are computers, servers, mobile devices, and networking infrastructure.

Software

Operating systems, productivity tools, programs, and gaming software.

Telecommunications

Internet service, wireless networks, and data transmission systems.

Services

Cloud architecture, like MongoDB Atlas, IT tech support, and cybersecurity protocols like HTTPS.

Real-World Influence

Impact of ICT

1. Efficiency

Through the automation of tasks and processes, manual effort is reduced. ICT enables organizations and individuals to complete complex workflows faster and with better quality.

2. Connectivity

Modern communication and collaboration bypasses boundaries, especially geogrpahical ones, allowing teams to work together in real time, without having to be physically present in one physical workspace, improving work and output quality
