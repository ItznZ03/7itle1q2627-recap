ICT Hazards & 5S Environment

Risk Assessment Spectrum

Common ICT Hazards

High-Voltage Hazards Extreme danger

Exposed CRT monitors and power supply units retaining dangerous, high-voltage electrical charges even after being disconnected.

Electrical Hazards High danger

Internal hardware components actively connected to live power currents that pose severe electrocution risks.

Chemical Hazards Moderate danger

Exposure to cleaning solvents, compressed gas dusters, and specialized keyboard cleaning chemicals.

Mechanical Hazards Moderate danger

Sharp chassis edges, internal metal frames, or moving cooling fans capable of causing physical cuts or pinches.

Physical Hazards Low danger

Loose cables trailing across walkways or wet, slippery floors presenting immediate trip and fall risks.

Workplace Organization

The 5S Methodology

Term English Action / Objective
Seiri Sort Clear away unnecessary items; keep only essential tools at the workstation.
Seiton Set in Order Arrange items systematically so every tool has an assigned position.
Seiso Shine Clean and remove dust, debris, and clutter from work surfaces regularly.
Seiketsu Standardize Maintain organized working conditions through uniform practices.
Shitsuke Sustain Build long-term discipline to maintain safety and organizational standards consistently.
