ICT Trends & Emerging Issues

Industry Developments

Key ICT Trends

1. Technological Convergence

Convergence allows a single device to perform multiple tasks, saving both space and energy. For example, modern smartphones combine web browsing, file management, document printing, and direct communication into one system.

2. Social Media

Social media platforms allow users to create, share, and exchange ideas online. Services such as Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, and YouTube showcase how individuals stay connected through shared content.

3. Mobile Technologies

Mobile technology uses portable hardware—such as laptops, tablets, and smartwatches—to improve daily efficiency. These devices support essential functions, including navigation, messaging, productivity apps, and video conferencing.

4. Assistive Technology

Assistive technology helps individuals with disabilities perform daily tasks independently. Examples include hearing aids, mobility equipment, screen readers, and adaptive communication tools.

Critical Challenges

Emerging ICT Issues

1. Cybercrime

Cybercrime involves illegal activities conducted online, such as financial fraud, cyberstalking, hacking, content piracy, and explicit content distribution. These security risks compromise user privacy and threaten network safety.

2. Digital Addiction

Digital addiction stems from the excessive or improper use of electronic devices and online platforms. Over time, unmanaged screen usage can negatively affect daily routines and overall well-being.

3. Health & Safety Concerns

Extended technology use can lead to physical issues such as digital eye strain, neck pain, and poor posture. Remaining in stationary positions for long periods often causes joint discomfort and physical fatigue.

4. Erosion of Social Skills

Overreliance on virtual communication can weaken real-world social interaction skills. Excessive screen time reduces opportunities for meaningful face-to-face conversations.
